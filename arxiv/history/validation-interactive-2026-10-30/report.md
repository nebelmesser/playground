The initial 16-model check passed **68/68 tests**. The current full-suite command is `node --test arxiv/models.test.mjs arxiv/*/*.test.mjs`; the flow-marker follow-up was checked separately below. Output: [validation/tests.txt](validation/tests.txt). The unchanged thirteen models were checked together this turn with `node arxiv/validate.mjs` before integrating the additions (65 runs). The final chemical-echolocation model and porous-displacement model were checked with focused `validate.mjs` commands (five runs each). The film's five full 96² runs were performed by its implementing subagent, including additional energy/volume measurements; its exact output is preserved in [validation/written-wetting-energy.jsonl](validation/written-wetting-energy.jsonl). These five records were incorporated into the combined JSONL with their provenance, without repeating the same expensive runs.

- **Chemical echolocation:** emission minus outlet drainage equals the chemical remaining in water. Tests cover a bent-channel physical-regression fixture, history-dependent future paths from identical geometry/placement, a blocked versus open tunnel, parameter corners and repeated tools. Both the Gaussian source and sensing footprint respect thin reflecting walls, including corner crossings; a one-cell-wall regression checks emission, gradient and actual motion. Brightness uses one fixed logarithmic concentration scale, so a remote concentration maximum cannot recolour unchanged water.
- **Writable wetting:** paired same-seed runs start with identical height and volume; wettable and repellent strokes cause opposite subsequent transfer. The actual film ruptures toward its precursor and collects raised liquid. The five full-grid 1,400-step runs preserve volume within 4×10⁻¹¹, remain positive and never increase the discrete energy at a fixed substrate. Drawing or changing substrate chemistry explicitly supplies external work.
- **Porous displacement:** tests check actual Darcy face-flux divergence, separately counted water input/output, deterministic paused tools, a developing ridge shadow and increased through-flow after opening a gap. In the paired scenario, the ridge reduces water behind it by about 25% and increases arrival near its ends by about 52%. Preset/corner runs advance 500 steps; additional interactive corner checks and a 1,000-step checkpoint remained conservative with zero bounding correction. The coarse solver broadens thin fingers and does not calibrate the paper's finger widths or trapped-oil fraction.

These checks establish numerical and interaction behavior of reduced browser models, not quantitative reproduction of all source experiments. Natural settling, finite domains, external interventions and approximation limits are disclosed on each model page.
